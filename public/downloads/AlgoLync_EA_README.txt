ALGOLYNX INSTITUTIONAL EXPERT ADVISOR (MT5)
===========================================
1. Place your compiled AlgoLync_EA.ex5 file inside MQL5/Experts/
2. In MetaTrader 5, open Tools -> Options -> Expert Advisors
3. Check "Allow WebRequest for listed URL" and add: https://fidulync.vercel.app
4. Attach the EA to your chart and paste your ALGOLYNX- license key into InpLicenseKey.
